KEEP ACCESS IN YOUR HANDS

Your link is the key.

Anyone with your active Port8080 link can control the host computer. Treat it like a password: share it privately, and stop the host when you’re done.

Performance depends on the host hardware and network. The 1080p/60 goal is a target, not a guarantee. A good connection helps keep the stream smooth.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before your first session.

What do I need?

A Mac or Windows host, Chrome on the viewer, and an internet connection. macOS may ask for screen recording and accessibility permissions.

Does it work on every network?

Some networks block direct WebRTC connections. Port8080’s first release may not connect on those networks.

Is the link safe to post publicly?

No. The active link grants control of the host to anyone who has it. Only share it with someone you trust.
